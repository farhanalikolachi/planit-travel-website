Gangnam is a vibrant district known for its upscale shopping, trendy restaurants, and lively nightlife. It is a prime location for visitors looking to experience the modern side of Seoul, with attractions such as the COEX Mall, Bongeunsa Temple, and the famous Gangnam Style statue.
