The National Mall is the heart of Washington, D.C., where some of the world’s most iconic and historic landmarks are located just a short stroll from each other. From the White House and the Capitol Building to the Lincoln Memorial, Washington Monument, and the Smithsonian Museums, this area leaves visitors in awe on a truly grand scale. Despite being the political epicenter of the United States and a top tourist destination, the area manages to maintain a peaceful and open atmosphere. Its wide avenues, green parks, and reflective pools make it ideal for walking and sightseeing. With a diverse range of hotels, cafés, and restaurants to suit all budgets and tastes, the National Mall offers a memorable and enriching experience.
